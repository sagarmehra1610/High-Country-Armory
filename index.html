Our Story

Veteran-Owned.
Expert-Led.

⚑ Alec Henkelman · 82nd Airborne · Owner & Operator

High Country Armory was built by Alec Henkelman — a U.S. Army veteran who deployed to Iraq with a reconnaissance unit in the 82nd Airborne, then earned a gunsmithing degree and turned his passion into Denver's go-to armory.

We believe every responsible person deserves expert, judgment-free guidance. Whether you're a first-time buyer or a seasoned collector, you'll always leave better informed — and probably with a great new firearm.
